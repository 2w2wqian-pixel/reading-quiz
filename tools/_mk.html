P.5 English Reading Assessment (First Term)

Reading Text

Tom lives in a small village near the sea. Every morning he walks to school with his sister Amy. They like to watch the fishing boats coming back to the harbour. One day, Tom found a small dog near the beach. It was wet and cold, so he took it home.

His mother said, "We can keep it if nobody is looking for it." Tom put up a notice at the village shop. A week later, an old man came and said the dog was his. Tom was sad but happy that the dog had a home.
